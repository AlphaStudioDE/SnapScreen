# SnapScreen

## Your printer. A more inspiring experience.

SnapScreen brings a clear, expressive touch experience to Snapmaker U1.
From preparing a print to watching the final layer, keep the information and
controls that matter close at hand.

**More creative freedom. Less interruption. One beautifully organized workspace.**

## Create in more colors

Explore multi-color printing with up to **16 colors** in supported configurations.
Keep materials in view and follow your creative workflow through one coherent
interface. Advanced material features remain a development preview while
real-world testing continues.

## Everything you need, beautifully presented

- **See the whole picture.** Follow temperatures, progress and remaining time
  from a dashboard shaped around your workflow.
- **Stay in control.** Reach everyday printer actions through a touch-first
  experience designed for the workshop.
- **Preview with confidence.** Browse jobs and explore print previews before
  starting your next project.
- **Keep an eye on the result.** Check the camera view and inspect the details
  that matter without interrupting your flow.
- **Make it yours.** Arrange your dashboard and choose an appearance that fits
  your printer, your workspace and the way you like to work.
- **Keep improving.** Receive new SnapScreen releases through the built-in
  update experience.

## Two editions. One familiar experience.

**SnapScreen Standard** offers a compact, practical printer-side experience.
**SnapScreen XL** provides a roomier view for users who want more screen space.
Choose the firmware intended for your edition; never interchange the two.
